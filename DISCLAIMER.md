# A note on what this is — and is not

**What Is Alive** is a research visualization and a philosophical instrument. It asks how aliveness, consciousness, and death might be drawn as layered, continuous, and measurable. It is not an answer a hospital can use.

- This is **not a medical device**.
- This is **not clinical guidance**. It does not diagnose, determine death, or recommend resuscitation, treatment, or end-of-life decisions.
- Charts, sliders, Φ estimates, HB₀ thresholds, and “protocol” language are **theoretical / illustrative**. They are not calibrated instruments and not patient data.
- Citations to published work (Borjigin, Xu, OrganEx, IIT) are invitations to read the papers. They are not a claim that this dashboard validates those findings, or that those findings validate this dashboard.

**Project Confluence** is a separate computational oncology research line (in silico). Mention of Confluence here is conceptual kinship — shared questions about complexity and Φ-collapse — **not** a clinical validation of this dashboard, and not a claim that Confluence has been clinically validated by this visualization.

The question remains the same: *what does it mean to be alive?*
This dashboard is an attempt to visualize the question. It is not a determination of the answer in any living person.
